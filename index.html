    About

    I started with a phone and a dark sky in Italy, and kept asking what the sensor was really recording.

    Tracking, calibration, and longer integration turned out to recover far more than I expected. I like understanding each step, from the sensor to the stack to the final image.

    I'm interested in imaging, experimentation, and aerospace. If you work on any of those and want to talk about a stack or a processing choice, I'd like to hear from you.
