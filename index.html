    About

    Hi, I'm Sanjeev Patel, a high school senior from San Diego with a love for photographing the night sky.

    I started in my backyard with a Sony α6000 and a zoom lens, taking short, untracked exposures and stacking them to bring out details I couldn't see in a single frame. I later added a Star Adventurer GTi tracking mount, which let me take longer exposures of galaxies and nebulae. Most recently, I upgraded to a William Optics RedCat 51 telescope, which I use with the same α6000.

    What keeps me coming back is watching an image come together, from setting up under the stars to bringing out faint details on my computer. I enjoy experimenting with how I process each photograph, finding a balance between the detail in the data and how I want the final image to feel. This website is a place to share those images and what I've learned along the way.
