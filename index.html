    About

    Hi, I'm Sanjeev Patel, a high school senior from San Diego with a love for photographing the night sky.

    After taking Photography 1-2 in my junior year of high school, I attempted to photograph the stars with my Sony α6000 and a zoom lens, taking short, untracked exposures and stacking them to bring out details I couldn't see in a single frame. I later added a Star Adventurer GTi tracking mount, which let me take longer exposures of galaxies and nebulae. Most recently, I upgraded to a William Optics RedCat 51 telescope, which I use with the same α6000.

    I love experimenting with how I process each dataset, as each requires a slightly different touch; finding a balance between the detail in the data and how I want the final image to feel is my goal as an astrophotographer. This website is a place for me to share the images I've captured, what I've learned along the way, and a few other cool visuals.
